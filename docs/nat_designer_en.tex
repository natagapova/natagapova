\section*{About}

I enjoy designing digital products where structure, visuals, and user flows work together. What I do best is taking an idea all the way to a concrete interface --- with flows, states, and details thought through so developers have fewer open questions at handoff. I work both in a studio team and independently on freelance projects --- from brief and wireflows to final layouts, graphics, and handoff to build.
